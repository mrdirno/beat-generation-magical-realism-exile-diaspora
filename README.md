## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a character 'listens' to a mundane object (a brick, a spoon, a coat) and hears the trapped echo of a historical event.
- Create a profile for a new client who hires Leo and Anya to find a 'missing sound' from their own diasporic past.
- Describe the sonic landscape of a place that is being actively silenced—gentrifying, bombed, or culturally erased.
- Write from the perspective of István, the café owner, as he observes the duo and the other 'ghosts' that visit his establishment.

### Technical Entry Points

- Design a speculative interface for 'sonic archaeology'—a device or software for visualizing and cataloging trapped sounds.
- Outline a 'Field Guide to Urban Palimpsests' that details methods for non-magical historical eavesdropping in layered cities.
